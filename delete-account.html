BarterHub Account Deletion Request

Use this page to understand how to request deletion of your BarterHub account and what records may be retained after your request is approved.

How To Request Account Deletion

  1. Open BarterHub.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Security Settings.
  4. Tap Request Account Deletion.

What Will Be Deleted

After your request is reviewed and approved, your account and profile data will be deleted.

Records We May Retain

Transaction records may be retained for legal, fraud prevention, security, and accounting purposes.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.
