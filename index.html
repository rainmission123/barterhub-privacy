BarterHub Support

Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your BarterHub account and personal data from inside the BarterHub app or by contacting our support team.

Delete Your Account In The App

  1. Open the BarterHub app.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile, then open Settings.
  4. Open Security.
  5. Tap Request Account Deletion.
  6. Confirm your request.

Request By Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email from the same email address used for your BarterHub account.

Data We Delete

  • Account profile details such as name, username, and bio.
  • Public profile information.
  • Profile photo and uploaded ID verification files, when available.
  • Device notification tokens.
  • Private wallet and premium status fields.

Data We May Retain

Some records may be retained when needed for fraud prevention, payment disputes, safety, legal obligations, or transaction history. These may include purchase records, coin transactions, reports, trade history, and messages connected to other users.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days. We may contact you if we need to verify ownership of the account.
