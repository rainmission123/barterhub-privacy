BarterHub PH Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

BarterHub PH values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared with service providers, and how you may request access, correction, or deletion of your account and personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BarterHub PH and the permissions you grant, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Account information: full name, username, email address, profile photo, login provider details, and account settings.
  • User-generated content: item listings, item photos, descriptions, categories, trade details, reports, reviews, and other content you submit in the app.
  • Chat and messaging data: messages, conversation metadata, timestamps, sender and recipient identifiers, and related trade communication.
  • Location information: approximate or precise location when you grant location permission, such as for map, distance, discovery, or listing-related features.
  • Device and app information: device identifiers, operating system, app version, diagnostics, crash logs, analytics data, usage events, and similar technical information.
  • Notification information: Firebase Cloud Messaging notification tokens and notification delivery preferences.
  • Wallet and coin activity: wallet balances, coin transactions, premium status, reward activity, purchase history, and related ledger records.
  • Payment-related transaction information: payment status, transaction references, amount, date, and other payment metadata processed through PayMongo. BarterHub PH does not store full card numbers or full card security codes.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and maintain user accounts.
  • Show user profiles, item listings, photos, descriptions, and trade-related content.
  • Enable chat, messaging, trades, item discovery, wallet, coin, premium, and support features.
  • Process payment-related transactions and update wallet or premium records.
  • Send service notifications, trade updates, account alerts, and other app messages.
  • Provide location-based or map-related functionality when permission is granted.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, spam, policy violations, and safety issues.
  • Analyze app performance, diagnose bugs, improve reliability, and develop new features.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, dispute, and platform policy obligations.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or process data with service providers that help us operate, secure, analyze, monetize, and improve BarterHub PH. These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Firebase

We use Firebase services for authentication, database storage, cloud features, analytics, crash reporting, security, and Firebase Cloud Messaging notifications.

Google Play Services

Google Play Services may process device, app, diagnostics, purchase, integrity, security, and account-related information to support Android app functionality and Google Play platform services.

AdMob

AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, ad interaction data, and analytics information to serve and measure ads, subject to your device settings and consent choices where applicable.

Facebook Login

If you sign in with Facebook, Facebook may provide authentication information such as your name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier, depending on your Facebook settings and permissions.

Cloudinary

Cloudinary may process uploaded media such as profile photos, item photos, listing images, and related image metadata so the app can store, optimize, and deliver images.

PayMongo

PayMongo processes payment-related information for purchases and transactions. BarterHub PH may receive transaction references, payment status, amount, and related metadata, but does not store full card details.

Mapbox

Mapbox may process location, map interaction, device, and usage data when you use map, distance, or location-based features in the app.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide BarterHub PH, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce our policies, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, and support business operations.

Some data may be deleted when you remove content or request account deletion. Certain records, such as payment, wallet, coin, safety, report, dispute, fraud-prevention, legal, or transaction records, may be retained for a longer period when required or reasonably necessary.

6. Security Measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, authentication, encrypted transport where supported, provider security features, monitoring, and limited access to systems that store user data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage users to keep account credentials private and to contact us immediately if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, update, object to certain processing, or request deletion of your personal information. You may also manage some permissions, such as location, notifications, advertising, and app data permissions, through your device settings.
